Benchmark

Leakage audit

v1 as reported vs honest evaluation

Mean absolute error in SOH points. The v1 number looked excellent because the answer was an input and the test rows had been seen in training.

What went wrong in v1

  1. The answer was an input. SOH is defined as 100 × capacity ÷ C0, and capacity was fed to the model. Within one battery the “prediction” was a fixed rescaling of an input.
  2. The test set overlapped training. Most of the reported test rows were cycles the model had already been trained on, so the score measured memory, not generalisation to a new cell.
  3. Proxies rebuilt the label. Whole-record statistics such as record length and mean current reconstruct capacity almost exactly, so dropping the capacity column alone does not remove the leak.

What v2 does instead

  • Inputs are health indicators measured inside a partial window of a charge or discharge. Capacity and full-discharge statistics are refused by the feature registry.
  • Every metric is leave-one-battery-out: the evaluated cell is never seen in training or calibration.
  • Intervals are calibrated on held-out batteries, and their empirical coverage is reported next to the error.

All audit evaluations

“Leaky input” marks evaluations that feed the model capacity or full-discharge statistics. R² is pooled over the evaluated cycles.
